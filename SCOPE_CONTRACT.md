# The 20-Day Plan — One Credible Artifact

*Written before the work. This is the pre-registered scope. Treat it as
Kira's constitution and Rung 1's pre-registration were treated: the WILL-NOT
list is binding, and any expansion must be a deliberate, dated amendment to
this document — never a quiet drift.*

---

## THE COMMITMENT

20 days. Every day, in a row. The terms are set and not under discussion.

## WHAT "A REAL THING" MEANS — AND DOES NOT

It does NOT mean three mature scientific platforms with strong claims. That
will not happen in 20 days, and pretending it could is the first inflation.

It means: **make one project externally credible, one project supporting, one
project frozen.** Pushing all three equally produces more surface area, more
half-claims, and more audit debt — the exact failure pattern the prior work
already shows.

The honest target is a **credible, inspectable, unusually honest computational
science artifact.** Not a Nature paper. Not a biotech company. Not a finished
platform. A thing people can open, run, and trust.

---

## THE RANKING (decided, with reasons)

**1. Physics-auditor — the flagship. Best 20-day shot.**
It can become a rigorous audit/reproducibility tool *without* wet-lab validation
or new biological data — and with good evidence, a partnership can follow later.
Crucially, it is the one project where the governance theme IS the product: a
tool that adjudicates other systems' confident structural claims and refuses
when it cannot see enough. Narrow it to a small defensible set of checks, real
examples, clean outputs, and explicit withdrawn/deferred claims, and it becomes
something people can inspect and trust.

**2. Kira (Rwanda AMR arm) — supporting. Real only if narrowed hard.**
Kira as broad computational drug discovery is too big and too fragile for 20
days, given the prior wrong-data/provenance history. But one arm can be real:
the Rwanda AMR tooling as a **data-quality / data-return kit kept as tooling,
not findings.** Kira's danger is sounding bigger than its evidence. The 20-day
job is to make it smaller, cleaner, more credible — not larger.

**3. Rung 1 (and any Rung 2+) — deferred. A focused methods artifact only.**
Rung 1 can be real if the baseline defect is fully corrected (it is), the
evaluation is reproducible, and claims are framed as "what this benchmark shows
under these controls" — not broad predictive intelligence. Cleanly document the
current state; do NOT let it consume the month unless it is already close. It is
not the main horse.

---

## THE REAL OPPORTUNITY — THE THEME

Across all three projects there is a pattern, built accidentally but with effort,
that is more valuable than "three projects": **scientific claim governance under
computational uncertainty.**

The science is not "we found the cure" or "we solved prediction." It is:

> How do computational systems prevent themselves from turning bad labels,
> stale data, weak baselines, and incomplete evidence into overconfident
> conclusions?

That is a serious, timely theme — and there is a worked evidence base for it:
five documented claim-walkbacks and five contamination catches, with the git
history to prove each one. The failures are not embarrassments to hide; they are
the evidence.

**The discipline on the theme:** it is the *lens* in the discussion section, NOT
the headline claim. The headline is the tool and what it concretely does. The
moment "a framework for scientific claim governance" becomes the headline, it
inflates — and committing that inflation would be both fatal and ironic, given
what the theme is about. Keep the theme as connective tissue; keep the tool as
the claim.

- **Secondary — Kira Rwanda AMR arm:** a grounded applied example of
  provenance / data-completeness tooling. (Provenance fix already shipped on
  `feat/amr-report-provenance`.)

## A REALISTIC 20-DAY WIN

- one clean repo that runs from scratch;
- one short technical report or preprint-style manuscript;
- one reproducible demo;
- no inflated claims;
- all synthetic data clearly marked;
- every real-data claim tied to source / provenance;
- all known failures documented instead of hidden;
- a clear "what this does / does not prove" section.

That is a real thing.

---

## THE BIGGEST RISK

Not effort. **Scope addiction.** The work already shows signs of ambition
outrunning evidence. The arithmetic of the month:

- 20 days **adding features** → lose (more surface area, more half-claims, more
  audit debt).
- 20 days **cutting claims, locking provenance, making one path reproducible,
  and writing the story clearly** → may come out with something genuinely solid.

---

## THE SCOPE CONTRACT (binding)

This artifact WILL contain:
- physics-auditor narrowed to a small, defensible, fully-scored set of checks;
- real example structures with clean, reproducible outputs;
- explicit withdrawn/deferred claims and a "what this does / does not prove"
  section;
- the Rwanda AMR arm as honest data-quality tooling (already provenance-safe);
- Rung 1 documented as a bounded methods artifact;
- one honest manuscript and one reproducible demo.

This artifact WILL NOT contain (without a dated amendment to this file):
- any new physics check beyond the defensible scored set; no H-bonds;
- any wet-lab claim or implication of experimental validation;
- any Kira drug-discovery, "cure," or compound-conditioned-prediction claim;
- Rung 2 or any new reprogramming rung;
- any new data source unless it clears a provenance bar set BEFORE looking;
- the governance theme stated as a headline claim rather than a discussion lens;
- "realistic" synthetic data — synthetic stays obviously synthetic.

Any temptation to cross the WILL-NOT line is handled by editing this contract
first, with a date and a reason — not by quiet drift. If it is not worth amending
the contract in writing, it is not worth doing.

---

## ONE INPUT THAT AFFECTS OUTPUT QUALITY

This is judgment work, not throughput work. Judgment degrades with fatigue in a
way invisible from the inside — and the moments that made the prior work real
(the boundary-tie adjudication, the wrong-label catches) required a clear head.
The relentless schedule is set; the only refinement is: stop when judgment goes
soft rather than pushing through it. That version produces a better artifact than
the grind-through-everything version.
